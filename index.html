Framing for This Call
The goal — what "success" looks like

This is a first meeting — the goal is connection and setting up an ongoing relationship, not a technical deep-dive. Ananya should walk in aiming to leave with:

  1. Sherry having a clear, memorable picture of who she is
  2. A concrete idea of what "being guided by Sherry" could look like practically
  3. A defined next step (not left vague)
⭐ The one-sentence summary

Make Sherry think: "this person is genuinely curious, doing interesting systems-meets-ML work, and would be easy and rewarding to mentor."
